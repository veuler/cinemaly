A visited countries map is useful because it gives your travel history a shape.
Instead of remembering trips as scattered photos or a list of city names, you
can see the places you have been on a world map.

Cinemaly's World tab is built for that exact job. Add the cities and countries
you have visited, watch them light up on the map, and keep simple travel stats
as your personal map fills in.

<Callout>
  The World tab is different from a cinematic trip capsule. Capsules tell the
  story of one journey. The World tab shows the bigger picture of everywhere
  you have been.
</Callout>

## What You Can Track

The World tab is designed around three simple pieces of travel memory:

- Places you have visited
- Countries represented by those places
- Progress stats that summarize how much of each country or the world you have seen

That makes it useful after a road trip, a backpacking route, a year of city
breaks, or a lifetime of travel you want to organize.

## How to Build Your Map

<Step num="01" title="Open the World tab">
  Start in Cinemaly's World tab. You will see a world map and an input for
  adding visited places.
</Step>

<Step num="02" title="Add a city or country">
  Type a place you have visited. Cinemaly uses map and place search to resolve
  the location, then adds it to your visited list.
</Step>

<Step num="03" title="Watch the map fill in">
  When boundary data is available, Cinemaly colors the visited area on the map.
  If a boundary is not available, the app can still show a point fallback for
  the place.
</Step>

<Step num="04" title="Use the stats panel">
  The stats panel summarizes visited places, countries, and country-level
  progress. It helps turn a simple list of cities into a visual travel record.
</Step>

## Privacy Notes

Cinemaly does not require an account, login, or backend profile for the World
tab. Your personal travel content is kept on your device. Place search may send
the location query to Nominatim/OpenStreetMap.
