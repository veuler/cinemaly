## Why Cinemaly Is the Better Travel Photo Map

Automatic photo mapping treats every location record as equally meaningful.
Cinemaly lets you make the decisions that create an actual story:

- Which cities and places belong in the final route?
- Which photos represent each stop?
- What note explains why the location mattered?
- In what order should someone experience the journey?
- How should the trip be kept after it is shared?

A week-long trip with 1,800 photos rarely needs 1,800 pins. It may need eight
carefully chosen stops, 40 meaningful photographs, and a few sentences that
bring those places back to life.

## Why Automatic Mapping Is Not Enough

[PhotoRoute](https://photoroute.app/) is centered on reading location metadata
from photos. That can quickly produce pins, but GPS data does not know which
moments mattered or how the trip should be told.

[anim8map](https://anim8map.com/) and
[TravelAnimator](https://www.travelanimator.com/) can place photos inside route
animations, but an image appearing in a video is not the same as preserving it
inside a reusable travel archive.

Cinemaly gives you editorial control and long-term ownership.
