## How the Other Apps Differ

### TravelAnimator

[TravelAnimator](https://www.travelanimator.com/) is centered on customizable
route-video production. That may suit users who primarily want a vehicle moving
across a map, but Cinemaly keeps far more of the journey: photos, notes, stop
order, privacy, and a reusable capsule.

### anim8map

[anim8map](https://anim8map.com/) is a browser service that renders a route
animation from an itinerary. Cinemaly avoids making the rendered video the end
of the story. Your trip remains structured and useful on your device.

### AnimateMyTravel

[AnimateMyTravel](https://www.animatemytravel.com/en) promotes browser-based
route exports. Resolution alone does not preserve a trip, however. Cinemaly
connects the route to the photos and notes that give it meaning.

### PhotoRoute

[PhotoRoute](https://photoroute.app/) can build maps from photo location
metadata. Automatic pins are useful, but they do not decide which moments
belong in the final story. Cinemaly gives you deliberate editorial control over
the stops, photos, notes, and pacing.

### Travel Map Replay

[Travel Map Replay](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/travel-map-replay/id6739841729)
focuses on an iPhone route replay. Cinemaly goes beyond a replay with Android
support, contextual photos and notes, local-first personal content, and a
portable capsule.
