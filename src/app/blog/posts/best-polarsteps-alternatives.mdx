Polarsteps is useful when you want live trip tracking and a polished travel log.
But not every traveler wants a live tracker, a social profile, or another place
where personal trip memories live in the cloud.

If you are looking for Polarsteps alternatives, the better question is not
"which app is the same?" It is "what kind of travel memory am I trying to make?"

<Callout>
  Cinemaly is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Polarsteps. It is
  a different kind of tool for private, cinematic post-trip memories.
</Callout>

## What to Look For in a Polarsteps Alternative

A good alternative should match your actual intent. Some people want live GPS
tracking. Some want itinerary planning. Some want a private recap after the
trip is over.

For private travel memories, look for:

- No required social feed
- Exportable or shareable output
- Clear privacy claims
- A format that preserves route order
- A way to include photos and notes together

## Where Cinemaly Fits

## Privacy Notes

Cinemaly does not upload your capsules, photos, notes, or videos to a Cinemaly
server. Your personal travel content is created and kept on your device. Place
search may send the location query to Nominatim/OpenStreetMap.
