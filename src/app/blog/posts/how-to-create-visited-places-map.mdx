## What a Visited Places Map Should Do

A useful visited places map should answer three simple questions:

- Where have I been?
- Which cities and countries matter most in my travel history?
- Which trips deserve a richer story with photos, notes, and a route?

That is why Cinemaly separates the big-picture map from individual travel
capsules. The map gives you overview. Capsules give you memory.

## Step 1: Add the Cities and Countries You Have Visited

<Step num="01" title="Open the World tab">
  Start with the map view that tracks your broader travel history. This is the
  best place to mark cities visited on a map without building a full trip
  capsule every time.
</Step>

<Step num="02" title="Search for a place">
  Add a city, country, or meaningful stop. Cinemaly uses place search to resolve
  the location, then adds it to your visited list.
</Step>

<Step num="03" title="Watch the map fill in">
  As you add places, Cinemaly helps you build a visual record of where you have
  been. When boundary data is available, countries can be highlighted; otherwise
  the place can still exist as a point on your map.
</Step>

## Step 2: Turn Important Routes Into Capsules

A visited places map is great for overview, but some journeys need more than a
checkmark. If a trip has a real path, use a Cinemaly capsule.

Capsules can keep:

1. The route and stop order
2. Photos attached to the places where they belong
3. Notes that explain why each stop mattered
4. A cinematic map story you can save or share

That means your map of places visited does not stay flat. The important trips
can become something you actually want to revisit.

## Privacy Notes

Cinemaly does not require an account, login, or social profile to create travel
memories. Personal capsule content is created and kept on your device unless
you choose to export or share it. Place search may send the location query to
Nominatim/OpenStreetMap.
